# Data Sources Disclosure

InvestiQ displays the source, retrieval time, market timestamp and delay status next to every market
value. Data sources the app can use (each must be enabled by the user; most require the user's own key):

| Source | Data | Typical delay | Terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alpha Vantage | Quotes, daily prices, company overview, FX, news | Delayed / end of day on free plans | alphavantage.co terms |
| Finnhub | Quotes, company profile and metrics, news, earnings calendar | Plan-dependent | finnhub.io terms |
| Twelve Data | Quotes, time series, symbol search | Plan-dependent | twelvedata.com terms |
| Financial Modeling Prep | Profiles (incl. ISIN), quotes, end-of-day prices, statements | End of day on free plan | FMP terms (personal licence on free plan) |
| Massive (formerly Polygon.io) | U.S. stock aggregates, reference data | End of day on free plan | massive.com terms |
| European Central Bank | Euro reference exchange rates, macro series | Daily (~16:00 CET) | ECB reuse policy |
| Czech National Bank | CZK exchange-rate fixing | Daily (working days) | CNB terms |
| FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis) | Macroeconomic series | Varies | FRED API terms; some series third-party copyrighted |
| U.S. SEC EDGAR | Financial statement facts of U.S. filers | Filing-based | SEC fair-access policy |
| Official RSS feeds (central banks, statistical offices, regulators) | Press releases and announcements | Minutes–hours | Publisher terms; headline, summary and link only |
| Demo data | Synthetic, generated by the app | n/a | Labelled "Demo data"; never mixed with real data |
| Manual entries | Values you type in | n/a | Labelled "Manual" |

Free data is never presented as real-time. When sources disagree, InvestiQ shows a warning and both values.
