## 1. Summary
InvestiQ is designed to work **locally**. Your profile, financial profile, portfolios, transactions,
budget, goals and learning progress are stored only on your Mac, encrypted, and are never sent to us.
We do not run servers that receive your data, we use no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and we
do not sell data.

## 2. Data processed on your device
Profile name, age category, country, preferred currency and language, onboarding answers, financial
records you enter, watchlists, app settings. Purpose: providing app functions. Legal basis: performance
of the service you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — processing happens on your device.

## 3. Data sent to third parties (only when you enable the feature)
| Recipient | Data | When |
|---|---|---|
| Market-data providers you configure (e.g. Alpha Vantage, Finnhub, Twelve Data, FMP, Massive) | Instrument symbols/ISINs you look up, your API key, technical data (IP address) | When you search or refresh data |
| ECB, Czech National Bank, FRED | Requested series, IP address | Exchange rates/macroeconomic data |
| U.S. SEC EDGAR | The contact e-mail you enter (required by SEC in the User-Agent), CIK of the company | Only if you enable SEC fundamentals |
| News feeds | Feed requests, IP address | When News is refreshed |
| External AI provider (optional) | Only the text you explicitly choose to send, minimised; no portfolio data unless you opt in per request | Only after explicit consent |
These recipients are independent controllers; their privacy policies apply.

## 4. Storage and security
Encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) with keys protected by your password (Argon2id) and the macOS Keychain.
Backups are encrypted with a passphrase you choose.

## 5. Your rights
Access/export (Settings → Data → Export), rectification (edit records), erasure (Settings → Delete
profile), restriction/objection (disable providers/AI). Because data stays on your device, you exercise
these rights directly in the app. You may lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data
Protection (ÚOOÚ) or your local supervisory authority.

## 6. Children
Users under 18 use an educational mode. External AI is disabled for them.
